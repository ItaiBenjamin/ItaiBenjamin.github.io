I'm an Electrical Engineering student at Virginia Commonwealth University, graduating Spring 2028. I spend most of my time somewhere between the code and the copper — writing firmware for microcontrollers, wiring up sensors, and figuring out why a signal looks wrong on the scope before it looks right.

Outside of coursework, I build things: robots that navigate on their own, circuits that shape sound, and the occasional piece of vintage hardware I probably shouldn't have opened up. I'm especially interested in embedded systems and low-level programming close to the hardware.

Feel free to reach out — my contact info is in the sidebar.
